NEPSEQuant

A research platform for understanding Nepal’s equity market. I used topological data analysis to identify four market regimes, then combined ensemble signals with risk-aware position sizing and walk-forward backtesting.

The engine accounts for local broker commissions, exchange fees, and capital gains tax, so the results reflect the friction of trading in NEPSE.
